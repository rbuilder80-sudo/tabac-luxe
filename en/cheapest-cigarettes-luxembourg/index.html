What makes up the price of a pack

In 2026, Luxembourg applies a proportional excise duty of 48.14% of the retail price on cigarettes, a fixed excise duty of €23.39 per 1,000 cigarettes, and 17% VAT. The minimum excise duty is €152.80 per 1,000 cigarettes. On a €7.70 pack of Marlboro, that is €4.17 of excise duty and €1.12 of VAT — €5.29 of taxes, 68.7% of the price. Draft budget 2027 (no. 8800) would raise the minimum excise duty to €165 per 1,000 cigarettes on 1 January 2027: a pack of Marlboro would then cost about €7.96 if the manufacturer passes on the rise. Sources: Administration des douanes et accises; rates and calculations published by cigprices.com in November 2026.
