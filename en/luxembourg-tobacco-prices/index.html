How prices are checked

The prices on this page are from Tabac Luxe's published price list, checked on 25 November 2026. The pack price is worked out by dividing the carton price by 10 (a 200-cigarette carton = 10 packs of 20), except for 40/160 formats, shown separately. Rolling tobacco is compared per gram, at the same weight. The in-store price is the final reference: it can change, including after the minimum excise rise due on 1 January 2027. French prices quoted are from the official list in force in November 2026.

What makes up the price of a pack

In 2026, Luxembourg applies a proportional excise duty of 48.14% of the retail price on cigarettes, a fixed excise duty of €23.39 per 1,000 cigarettes, and 17% VAT. The minimum excise duty is €152.80 per 1,000 cigarettes. On a €7.70 pack of Marlboro, that is €4.17 of excise duty and €1.12 of VAT — €5.29 of taxes, 68.7% of the price. Draft budget 2027 (no. 8800) would raise the minimum excise duty to €165 per 1,000 cigarettes on 1 January 2027: a pack of Marlboro would then cost about €7.96 if the manufacturer passes on the rise. Sources: Administration des douanes et accises; rates and calculations published by cigprices.com in November 2026.

How many cigarettes can I bring back to France from Luxembourg?

For personal use, French customs use 800 cigarettes as the indicative limit, along with 400 cigarillos, 200 cigars and 1 kg of rolling tobacco. Above those amounts you must be able to show the goods are for personal use. Prices on this page are for in-store purchases, collected in person.

Do the prices shown include tax?

Yes. The Luxembourg prices shown include 17% VAT and Luxembourg excise duty. The price shown is the price paid in store, with no extra charges — sales are in store with collection only, no online sales and no shipping.

What happens to prices on 1 January 2027?

Luxembourg's draft budget provides for a rise in the minimum excise duty, from €152.80 to €165 per 1,000 cigarettes, on 1 January 2027. Prices on this page are those of November 2026: they may be adjusted in store after that date, and the in-store price remains the final reference.
