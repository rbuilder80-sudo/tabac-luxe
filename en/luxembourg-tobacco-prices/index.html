Frequently asked questions

How much does tobacco cost in Luxembourg?

As of 25 November 2026 at Tabac Luxe: a pack of 20 cigarettes from €5.70 (Austin Red) to €8.80 (Dunhill International), Marlboro Red €7.70; a 50g pack of rolling tobacco from €6.20 (Springfield Yellow Virginia) to €13.30 (Amber Leaf). The in-store price is the final reference.

What is the cheapest rolling tobacco in Luxembourg?

The cheapest 50g is Springfield Yellow Virginia at €6.20, followed by Keops Blue and Keops Zwaar at €7.25 and The Turner Original at €7.30.

Are prices different for cross-border customers?

Published in-store prices apply to adult customers purchasing at the Luxembourg store. Customers are responsible for any rules that apply when taking products across a border.
