How prices are checked

The prices on this page are from Tabac Luxe's published price list, checked on 27 November 2026. The pack price is worked out by dividing the carton price by the number of packs in the carton; other formats are shown separately. The in-store price is the final reference. On the price, taxes weigh heavily: proportional excise duty of 48.14%, fixed excise duty of €23.39 per 1,000 cigarettes and 17% VAT — see the detail on the cheapest cigarettes in Luxembourg page. The draft 2027 budget would raise the minimum excise duty to €165 per 1,000 cigarettes on 1 January 2027 — about €0.24 more per pack of 20 if the rise is passed on in full.
