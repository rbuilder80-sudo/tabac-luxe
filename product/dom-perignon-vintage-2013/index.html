Dom Pérignon — Vintage 2013

Hautvillers, France

Only produced in exceptional years. The 2013 vintage opens with white fruit and floral notes, then reveals candied citrus, anise and a smoky minerality. A long, restrained finish — Dom Pérignon at its most architectural.

Dom Pérignon Vintage 2013 is produced only in exceptional years — the 2013 was declared after a particularly long, cool growing season that produced grapes of exceptional balance and acidity. The wine opens with white fruit and floral notes, then reveals candied citrus, anise, ginger and a smoky minerality. The finish is long and restrained, with a chalky, saline quality characteristic of the great Dom Pérignon vintages. A Champagne at its most architectural — built for ageing, but already drinking beautifully. Each bottle is presented in the iconic Dom Pérignon gift box.

About Dom Pérignon Dom Pérignon — Vintage 2013

Dom Pérignon Vintage 2013 is produced only in exceptional years — the 2013 was declared after a particularly long, cool growing season that produced grapes of exceptional balance and acidity. The wine opens with white fruit and floral notes, then reveals candied citrus, anise, ginger and a smoky minerality. The finish is long and restrained, with a chalky, saline quality characteristic of the great Dom Pérignon vintages. A Champagne at its most architectural — built for ageing, but already drinking beautifully. Each bottle is presented in the iconic Dom Pérignon gift box.

This champagne is part of Tabac Luxe's curated selection of champagne: vintage and prestige cuvée champagne from the grand maisons of épernay and reims — dom pérignon, cristal, krug.

Available exclusively in-store at our Luxembourg boutique on Rue des Capucins, Dom Pérignon — Vintage 2013 by Dom Pérignon represents the standard of luxury and craftsmanship that defines the Tabac Luxe collection. Our cellar master has personally selected this bottle for its character, provenance and craftsmanship. Please visit us to discover it in person — our team will be delighted to advise on pairings, storage and service.
