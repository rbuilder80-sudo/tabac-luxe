I — The Maison

A boutique devoted to the art of indulgence

✦

Tabac Luxe is Luxembourg's curated destination for the world's finest cigars, rare spirits and artisan chocolates. Every bottle, every cigar, every praliné is hand-selected by our cellar master — chosen not for prestige alone, but for character, story and craftsmanship. Our doors have welcomed connoisseurs and the simply curious since 2025.

III — A Note on Service

In-store only — by design

✦

We do not ship. We do not deliver. Every cigar, every bottle, every praliné is presented in our Luxembourg boutique, where our staff can advise on storage, pairings and provenance. Luxury of this calibre deserves a personal introduction — and you deserve the experience of selecting it in person. Please visit us during opening hours, or call ahead to reserve.
